Explore the options

Support for the person, not just the problem.

Polaris brings together therapy, assessment, BrainGym, developmental support, medical and complementary expertise. The right starting point depends on the individual.

Therapy & emotional wellbeing

Individual therapy, psychotherapy, counselling and support for anxiety, stress, mood, trauma, loss, identity, life changes and emotional wellbeing.

  • Individual psychology and therapy
  • Psychotherapy and counselling
  • EMDR and trauma-informed support
  • Mindfulness-based approaches
Relationships, couples & families

Support with relationships, communication, parenting, separation, family dynamics and times of change.

  • Couples and relationship therapy
  • Family therapy
  • Parent guidance and support
Children & young people

Psychological, developmental and therapeutic support shaped around the child or young person, with family involvement where useful.

  • Emotional and behavioural support
  • Developmental and neurodiversity support
  • Speech and occupational support
  • Parent and family work
Assessment & specialist support

Assessment can help build a clearer picture and guide useful next steps. Available services include psychological, psychometric, developmental, psychiatric and other specialist input.

  • ADHD, autism and neurodiversity
  • Psychological and psychometric assessment
  • Psychiatry and medical support
  • Occupational and speech-language assessment
Coaching, confidence & performance

Goal-focused support for personal development, career decisions, executive performance, confidence, neurodivergence and life transitions.

Body & physical wellbeing

Physical, lifestyle and complementary wellbeing services can sit alongside the wider Polaris offering when they are relevant to the person.

BrainGym

qEEG brain mapping and EEG-based neurofeedback provide an additional specialist perspective within Polaris.
